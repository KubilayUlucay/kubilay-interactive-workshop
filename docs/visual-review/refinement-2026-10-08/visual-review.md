# Independent visual review

The reviewer inspected actual source, all seven untouched original baseline PNGs,
fresh before captures, and new desktop/390px/360px renders. The lead retained sole
ownership of implementation.

Accepted requested corrections:

- Idle crank, housing, copper, printed support and platform are readable.
- Metal/copper/plastic finishes are distinct; the platform has a matte finish.
- Final mobile camera fits the entire rig and plinth with visible margins at
  390px and 360px, including powered, exploded and shaft-selected states.
- Component descriptions occupy their own mobile card below the geometry.
- Desktop scene/dock continuity is repaired, with the plinth above the dock.
- Selected shaft geometry and corresponding button/description are clear.

The reviewer rejected the blank first 360px powered PNG despite valid state
metadata. The lead synchronized test rendering/capture and recaptured all four
360px states. The reviewer inspected and accepted the replacement; metadata alone
was not treated as sufficient evidence.

Remaining artistic issues: the front exploded cover partly hides the crank;
selected stator support is pale; some shadows retain angular silhouettes.
Reference-level finish and real-device motion/performance remain unproven. These
captures are actual local software WebGL renders, not live-site screenshots.

Shorter desktop (1365×768): geometry and card/tool separation were accepted.
An initial powered screenshot omitted numeric glyphs; the reviewer inspected
the synchronized replacement and confirmed the complete 100% label. The
replacement uses the same downloaded fonts and a capture-only compositor hint.
